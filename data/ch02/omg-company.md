# OMG Consulting: Company History and Profile

## Foundation and Early Years (2005-2010)

OMG Consulting ("Optimize, Modernize, Grow") was founded in 2005 by three former enterprise software executives—Sarah Mitchell, David Chen, and Thomas Rodriguez—who recognized the growing need for specialized technology consulting in a rapidly evolving digital landscape. Headquartered in Boston with an initial team of just 12 consultants, the firm positioned itself as a boutique consultancy focused on helping mid-sized businesses optimize their IT infrastructure and business processes.

During these early years, OMG primarily served clients in the financial services and healthcare sectors in the Northeast United States. The company quickly established a reputation for its pragmatic approach and deep domain expertise, with a special focus on legacy system modernization and custom software development. By 2010, the company had grown to 35 employees and opened a second office in Chicago.

## Expansion Phase (2011-2017)

The advent of cloud computing created new opportunities for OMG, which rapidly developed capabilities in cloud migration and architecture. In 2011, the company established its Cloud Solutions practice, becoming an early partner of AWS and later Microsoft Azure. This period also saw OMG's geographic expansion with new offices in San Francisco (2013) and Austin (2015).

As the company grew, it diversified its service offerings to include data analytics, cybersecurity, and digital transformation consulting. The acquisition of DataMetrics, a boutique data science firm, in 2016 significantly enhanced OMG's capabilities in business intelligence and advanced analytics.

By 2017, OMG had grown to over 150 consultants working across multiple industry verticals including financial services, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, and the public sector. The company's success was built on its ability to blend strategic business consulting with technical implementation expertise.

## Recent History and Digital Transformation Focus (2018-Present)

Since 2018, OMG Consulting has sharpened its focus on helping clients navigate the complexities of digital transformation, with particular emphasis on AI, IoT, blockchain, and modern data platforms. The company reorganized its practice areas in 2019 to align with evolving client needs:

- **Enterprise Applications and Integration**
- **Cloud and Infrastructure Modernization**
- **Data Platforms and Analytics**
- **Cybersecurity and Risk Management**
- **Digital Experience and Customer Engagement**
- **Emerging Technologies (AI/ML, Blockchain, IoT)**

In 2020, despite the global pandemic, OMG successfully pivoted to a remote-first operating model and actually expanded its client base as organizations accelerated their digital transformation initiatives. The company made strategic investments in developing proprietary methodologies and frameworks to help clients implement digital solutions faster and more effectively.

The appointment of Elena Gomez as CEO in 2021 marked a new chapter for OMG, with renewed emphasis on building capabilities in artificial intelligence, knowledge graphs, and platform engineering. Under Gomez's leadership, the company has strengthened its partnerships with leading technology vendors including Neo4j, Google Cloud, Databricks, and Snowflake.

## Current Position and Future Direction

Today, OMG Consulting employs over 250 professionals across its five offices in the United States and serves clients globally. The company has been recognized as a "Leader" in several industry analyst reports for its work in data modernization and intelligent automation.

In the most recent fiscal year, OMG recorded revenue of $78 million, with a compound annual growth rate of 18% over the past five years. The company remains privately held, with the original founders maintaining significant ownership alongside a select group of senior partners.

Looking ahead, OMG is heavily investing in developing its capabilities around generative AI, knowledge graphs, and composable business architectures. The company's strategic plan includes:

- Deepening specialized industry expertise in healthcare, financial services, and manufacturing
- Expanding capabilities in AI ethics, governance, and responsible data use
- Developing reusable accelerators and IP around knowledge-centered AI solutions
- Expanding its talent base through both hiring and strategic acquisitions
- Opening international offices in Toronto and London

## Organizational Culture and Values

OMG Consulting prides itself on a collaborative culture that combines intellectual rigor with pragmatic problem-solving. The company's core values include:

- **Client Impact First**: Prioritizing measurable client outcomes over everything else
- **Intellectual Curiosity**: Continuously learning and exploring emerging technologies
- **Inclusive Innovation**: Bringing diverse perspectives to solve complex problems
- **Pragmatic Excellence**: Delivering practical solutions with exceptional quality
- **People Development**: Investing in the growth of consultants at all levels

The company maintains a strong commitment to diversity, with women making up 42% of its workforce and 38% of leadership positions. OMG has also established a robust campus recruiting program that builds relationships with top universities, with particular focus on supporting underrepresented groups in technology.

## Community and Industry Engagement

OMG Consulting maintains active involvement in technology communities and industry associations. The company regularly contributes to open-source projects, sponsors technology conferences, and publishes thought leadership content through its research arm, OMG Insights.

The company's "Tech for Good" initiative provides pro bono consulting services to nonprofit organizations, with a special focus on educational institutions and healthcare providers serving underserved communities. Since its inception in 2015, this program has contributed over $5 million in services to more than 30 organizations.

Through its evolution from a small regional player to a respected national consultancy, OMG Consulting has maintained its focus on delivering practical technology solutions that drive measurable business outcomes. As the company continues to grow, it remains committed to helping organizations harness the power of emerging technologies to optimize their operations, modernize their systems, and grow their businesses—staying true to the principles reflected in its name.
