# Financial Crime Prevention

## Project Overview

Advanced financial crime detection and prevention system using machine learning and graph analytics. This strategic initiative for Global Banking Corporation aimed to transform their operations in the Financial Services sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Global Banking Corporation is a leading organization in the Financial Services industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to risk & compliance. The project was conducted between 2021-01-15 and 2022-03-31 with a budget of 2700000-4100000.

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Fragmented customer experience across multiple touchpoints
- Balancing innovation with stability and risk management
- Legacy systems with minimal documentation and institutional knowledge gaps

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Global Banking Corporation:

- Modernized core systems, reducing technical debt by 39% and enabling future innovation
- Deployed 9 new AI/ML models that improved decision accuracy by 38%
- Successfully migrated 18 applications to the cloud with zero business disruption
- Increased operational efficiency by 24%, resulting in annual savings of $306K
- Accelerated time-to-market for new features by 64%, enabling faster response to market opportunities

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future risk & compliance projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
