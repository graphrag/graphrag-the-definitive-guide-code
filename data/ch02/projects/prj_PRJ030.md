# GraphRAG Knowledge System

## Project Overview

Internal knowledge management system using Graph-based RAG to improve consultant access to organizational knowledge. This strategic initiative for OMG Consultants aimed to transform their operations in the Professional Services sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

OMG Consultants is a leading organization in the Professional Services industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to knowledge management. The project was conducted between 2023-01-10 and 2023-08-31 with a budget of 800000-1500000.

## Team Composition

OMG Consulting deployed a cross-functional team of 5 consultants to execute this project.

- **Data Strategy**: Sarah Johnson (Data Platforms & Analytics) - Knowledge graph design, data modeling, analytics framework

## Technologies and Methodologies

This project utilized a modern technology stack including:

- Semantic Search
- GraphRAG
- LangChain
- Neo4j
- Entity Extraction
- Notion

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Balancing innovation with stability and risk management
- Evolving requirements and scope changes throughout the project lifecycle
- Fragmented customer experience across multiple touchpoints

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to OMG Consultants:

- Implemented automated workflows that reduced manual processes by 67%, freeing staff for higher-value work
- Increased operational efficiency by 39%, resulting in annual savings of $394K
- Implemented real-time analytics capabilities, reducing reporting latency from days to minutes
- Successfully migrated 22 applications to the cloud with zero business disruption

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future knowledge management projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
