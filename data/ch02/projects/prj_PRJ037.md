# Supply Chain Traceability

## Project Overview

Supply chain traceability platform using blockchain, IoT sensors, and analytics for end-to-end visibility. This strategic initiative for Global Consumer Goods aimed to transform their operations in the Consumer Goods sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Global Consumer Goods is a leading organization in the Consumer Goods industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to supply chain. The project was conducted between 2021-04-01 and 2022-07-31 with a budget of 2300000-3700000.

## Team Composition

OMG Consulting deployed a cross-functional team of 4 consultants to execute this project.

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Limited availability of subject matter experts for critical components
- Balancing innovation with stability and risk management
- Complex security and compliance requirements
- Siloed data across multiple departments requiring complex integration

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Global Consumer Goods:

- Expanded digital capabilities, resulting in 36% increase in digital channel adoption
- Increased operational efficiency by 30%, resulting in annual savings of $233K
- Achieved 3x improvement in system performance, handling 11K requests per minute at peak
- Accelerated time-to-market for new features by 38%, enabling faster response to market opportunities

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future supply chain projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
