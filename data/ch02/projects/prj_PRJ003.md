# Healthcare Patient Portal

## Project Overview

Redesign and implementation of patient portal with integrated telehealth capabilities, appointment scheduling, and medical records access. This strategic initiative for Northeast Medical Center aimed to transform their operations in the Healthcare sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Northeast Medical Center is a leading organization in the Healthcare industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to digital experience. The project was conducted between 2022-04-18 and 2023-02-28 with a budget of 1500000-2500000.

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Integration with multiple third-party systems with limited API support
- Evolving requirements and scope changes throughout the project lifecycle
- Geographic distribution of team members across multiple time zones

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Northeast Medical Center:

- Implemented automated workflows that reduced manual processes by 56%, freeing staff for higher-value work
- Modernized core systems, reducing technical debt by 28% and enabling future innovation
- Accelerated time-to-market for new features by 68%, enabling faster response to market opportunities
- Successfully migrated 23 applications to the cloud with zero business disruption
- Migrated 70TB of data to the cloud, reducing storage costs by 65% while improving accessibility

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future digital experience projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
