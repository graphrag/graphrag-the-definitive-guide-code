# Industrial Smart Factory

## Project Overview

Smart factory transformation with connected equipment, digital twins, and advanced analytics. This strategic initiative for Global Industrial Manufacturer aimed to transform their operations in the Manufacturing sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Global Industrial Manufacturer is a leading organization in the Manufacturing industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to industry 4.0. The project was conducted between 2022-07-15 and 2023-04-30 with a budget of 3000000-4500000.

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Resistance to change from key stakeholders and end users
- Fragmented customer experience across multiple touchpoints
- Legacy systems with minimal documentation and institutional knowledge gaps

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Global Industrial Manufacturer:

- Established a scalable platform that can support 172% user growth without significant additional investment
- Successfully migrated 20 applications to the cloud with zero business disruption
- Expanded digital capabilities, resulting in 32% increase in digital channel adoption

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future industry 4.0 projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
