# IoT Manufacturing Platform

## Project Overview

Smart factory implementation with IoT sensors, edge computing, and predictive maintenance capabilities. This strategic initiative for Advanced Manufacturing Co. aimed to transform their operations in the Manufacturing sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Advanced Manufacturing Co. is a leading organization in the Manufacturing industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to industry 4.0. The project was conducted between 2020-11-01 and 2022-01-31 with a budget of 3500000-5000000.

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Balancing innovation with stability and risk management
- Legacy systems with minimal documentation and institutional knowledge gaps
- Evolving requirements and scope changes throughout the project lifecycle

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Advanced Manufacturing Co.:

- Reduced system downtime by 95%, improving service reliability to 99.7% availability
- Modernized core systems, reducing technical debt by 38% and enabling future innovation
- Deployed 3 new AI/ML models that improved decision accuracy by 16%

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future industry 4.0 projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
