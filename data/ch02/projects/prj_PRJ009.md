# Healthcare Data Modernization

## Project Overview

Healthcare data platform modernization with focus on clinical analytics, population health, and regulatory compliance. This strategic initiative for Midwest Health Systems aimed to transform their operations in the Healthcare sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Midwest Health Systems is a leading organization in the Healthcare industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to data & analytics. The project was conducted between 2021-05-15 and 2022-09-30 with a budget of 2200000-3500000.

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Limited availability of subject matter experts for critical components
- Legacy system dependencies that couldn't be easily modernized
- Legacy systems with minimal documentation and institutional knowledge gaps
- Evolving requirements and scope changes throughout the project lifecycle

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Midwest Health Systems:

- Delivered a unified experience across 5 channels, increasing engagement metrics by 31%
- Achieved 3x improvement in system performance, handling 81K requests per minute at peak
- Improved customer satisfaction scores by 1.6 points, reaching an all-time high of 9.6/10
- Deployed 3 new AI/ML models that improved decision accuracy by 34%

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future data & analytics projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
