# Digital Employee Experience

## Project Overview

Digital workplace transformation with focus on collaboration tools, knowledge management, and employee self-service. This strategic initiative for Global Consulting Group aimed to transform their operations in the Professional Services sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Global Consulting Group is a leading organization in the Professional Services industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to employee experience. The project was conducted between 2021-08-15 and 2022-07-31 with a budget of 1700000-2800000.

## Team Composition

OMG Consulting deployed a cross-functional team of 4 consultants to execute this project.

- **Project Lead**: Nicole Adams (HR Technology & Transformation) - Employee experience strategy, digital workplace vision, change management
- **UX Design**: Michelle Wong (Digital Experience) - User experience design, information architecture, design system
- **Learning Experience**: Gabriela Santos (Learning & Development) - Knowledge management, learning platform, content strategy

## Technologies and Methodologies

This project utilized a modern technology stack including:

- API Gateway
- Angular
- Docker
- Azure Functions
- Microservices
- Node.js

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Significant technical debt and outdated infrastructure
- Fragmented customer experience across multiple touchpoints
- Siloed data across multiple departments requiring complex integration
- Aggressive timeline with limited resources and competing priorities

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Global Consulting Group:

- Enhanced data security compliance, successfully meeting PCI-DSS requirements with zero audit findings
- Unified customer view across all channels, increasing cross-sell opportunities by 17%
- Accelerated time-to-market for new features by 50%, enabling faster response to market opportunities

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future employee experience projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
