# Healthcare GraphRAG Implementation

## Project Overview

Implementation of GraphRAG-powered clinical knowledge platform integrating medical literature, guidelines, and EHR data. This strategic initiative for Massachusetts General Hospital aimed to transform their operations in the Healthcare sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Massachusetts General Hospital is a leading organization in the Healthcare industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to artificial intelligence. The project was conducted between 2023-02-15 and Active with a budget of 2500000-4000000.

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Legacy system dependencies that couldn't be easily modernized
- Evolving requirements and scope changes throughout the project lifecycle
- Geographic distribution of team members across multiple time zones
- Aggressive timeline with limited resources and competing priorities

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Massachusetts General Hospital:

- Deployed 10 new AI/ML models that improved decision accuracy by 16%
- Increased operational efficiency by 30%, resulting in annual savings of $175K
- Implemented automated workflows that reduced manual processes by 72%, freeing staff for higher-value work

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future artificial intelligence projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
