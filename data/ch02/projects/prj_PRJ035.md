# Healthcare Knowledge Management

## Project Overview

Healthcare knowledge management system for clinical protocols, best practices, and medical research integration. This strategic initiative for Regional Healthcare Provider aimed to transform their operations in the Healthcare sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Regional Healthcare Provider is a leading organization in the Healthcare industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to knowledge management. The project was conducted between 2020-06-15 and 2021-12-31 with a budget of 1800000-3000000.

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Strict regulatory compliance requirements in a rapidly changing landscape
- Limited availability of subject matter experts for critical components
- Fragmented customer experience across multiple touchpoints
- Performance and scalability challenges during peak usage periods

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Regional Healthcare Provider:

- Achieved 10x improvement in system performance, handling 32K requests per minute at peak
- Unified customer view across all channels, increasing cross-sell opportunities by 39%
- Accelerated time-to-market for new features by 31%, enabling faster response to market opportunities
- Improved customer satisfaction scores by 1.8 points, reaching an all-time high of 9.5/10

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future knowledge management projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
