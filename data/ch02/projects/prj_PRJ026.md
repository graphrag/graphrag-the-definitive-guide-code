# Knowledge Graph Implementation

## Project Overview

Enterprise knowledge graph implementation connecting research data, publications, and expertise. This strategic initiative for Global Research Institute aimed to transform their operations in the Education sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Global Research Institute is a leading organization in the Education industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to knowledge management. The project was conducted between 2021-07-01 and 2022-09-30 with a budget of 1900000-3100000.

## Team Composition

OMG Consulting deployed a cross-functional team of 4 consultants to execute this project.

### Key Team Members and Roles

- **Project Lead**: Sarah Johnson (Data Platforms & Analytics) - Knowledge graph strategy, ontology design, implementation planning
- **AI Integration**: Jason Chen (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) - NLP implementation, entity extraction, relationship modeling
- **Architecture Design**: Rachel Garcia (Enterprise Architecture) - System design, integration patterns, scalability architecture
- **Data Engineering**: Benjamin Okonkwo (Data Engineering) - Graph database implementation, data ingestion, query optimization


## Technologies and Methodologies

This project utilized a modern technology stack including:

- Entity Extraction
- Vector Databases
- SharePoint
- RAG Pipelines
- Notion
- GraphQL
- Knowledge Graphs
- Confluence

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Resistance to change from key stakeholders and end users
- Performance and scalability challenges during peak usage periods
- Fragmented customer experience across multiple touchpoints

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Global Research Institute:

- Accelerated time-to-market for new features by 37%, enabling faster response to market opportunities
- Enhanced data security compliance, successfully meeting ISO 27001 requirements with zero audit findings
- Increased operational efficiency by 24%, resulting in annual savings of $350K
- Successfully migrated 15 applications to the cloud with zero business disruption

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future knowledge management projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
