# HR Transformation

## Project Overview

HR technology transformation with cloud HCM implementation, employee experience, and workforce analytics. This strategic initiative for Enterprise Services Corp. aimed to transform their operations in the Professional Services sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Enterprise Services Corp. is a leading organization in the Professional Services industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to hr technology. The project was conducted between 2022-02-01 and 2023-03-15 with a budget of 2200000-3500000.

## Team Composition

OMG Consulting deployed a cross-functional team of 4 consultants to execute this project.

- **Experience Design**: Michelle Wong (Digital Experience) - Employee experience design, portal UX, service design

## Technologies and Methodologies

This project utilized a modern technology stack including:

- AWS Lambda
- React

- AWS CloudFront

- Docker

- Spring Boot
- Azure App Service
- Event-Driven Architecture

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Fragmented customer experience across multiple touchpoints
- Limited availability of subject matter experts for critical components
- Balancing innovation with stability and risk management

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Enterprise Services Corp.:

- Unified customer view across all channels, increasing cross-sell opportunities by 27%
- Implemented real-time analytics capabilities, reducing reporting latency from days to minutes
- Established a scalable platform that can support 83% user growth without significant additional investment
- Modernized core systems, reducing technical debt by 49% and enabling future innovation
- Reduced system downtime by 59%, improving service reliability to 99.5% availability

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future hr technology projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
