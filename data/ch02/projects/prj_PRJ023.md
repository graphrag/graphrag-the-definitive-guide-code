# Product Innovation Lab

## Project Overview

Corporate innovation lab establishment with focus on emerging technologies, venture building, and product incubation. This strategic initiative for Consumer Electronics Inc. aimed to transform their operations in the Manufacturing sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Consumer Electronics Inc. is a leading organization in the Manufacturing industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to innovation. The project was conducted between 2022-09-01 and 2023-06-30 with a budget of 2300000-3700000.

## Team Composition

OMG Consulting deployed a cross-functional team of 4 consultants to execute this project.

### Key Team Members and Roles

- **Project Lead**: Lisa Wang (Innovation & Emerging Technology) - Innovation strategy, lab design, operation model development
- **Product Strategy**: Carlos Rodriguez (Digital Product Strategy) - Product development framework, incubation methodology, commercialization
- **Design Thinking**: Michelle Wong (Digital Experience) - Innovation workshops, user research, concept development
- **Emerging Technology**: Jason Chen (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) - Technology assessment, proof-of-concept development, technology roadmap

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Data quality and governance issues affecting system reliability
- Legacy system dependencies that couldn't be easily modernized
- Legacy systems with minimal documentation and institutional knowledge gaps
- Geographic distribution of team members across multiple time zones

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Consumer Electronics Inc.:

- Successfully migrated 12 applications to the cloud with zero business disruption
- Reduced system downtime by 56%, improving service reliability to 99.7% availability
- Implemented real-time analytics capabilities, reducing reporting latency from days to minutes
- Modernized core systems, reducing technical debt by 31% and enabling future innovation
- Improved customer satisfaction scores by 1.7 points, reaching an all-time high of 8.6/10

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future innovation projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
