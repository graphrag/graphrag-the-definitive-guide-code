# Retail Omnichannel Platform

## Project Overview

Omnichannel commerce platform connecting in-store, online, and mobile experiences through event-driven architecture. This strategic initiative for National Retail Chain aimed to transform their operations in the Retail sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

National Retail Chain is a leading organization in the Retail industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to digital experience. The project was conducted between 2019-05-15 and 2020-09-30 with a budget of 2500000-4000000.

## Team Composition

OMG Consulting deployed a cross-functional team of 4 consultants to execute this project.

- Adobe Experience Manager

- UX Research
- Contentful

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Complex security and compliance requirements
- Data quality and governance issues affecting system reliability
- Limited availability of subject matter experts for critical components
- Legacy system dependencies that couldn't be easily modernized

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to National Retail Chain:

- Unified customer view across all channels, increasing cross-sell opportunities by 15%
- Successfully migrated 5 applications to the cloud with zero business disruption
- Implemented automated workflows that reduced manual processes by 70%, freeing staff for higher-value work
- Accelerated time-to-market for new features by 69%, enabling faster response to market opportunities
- Increased operational efficiency by 28%, resulting in annual savings of $340K

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future digital experience projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
