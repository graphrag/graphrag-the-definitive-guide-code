# Blockchain Supply Chain

## Project Overview

Blockchain implementation for pharmaceutical supply chain traceability, authenticity verification, and regulatory compliance. This strategic initiative for Pharmaceutical Innovations aimed to transform their operations in the Pharmaceuticals sector, leveraging cutting-edge technologies and methodologies to achieve significant business impact.

## Client Profile

Pharmaceutical Innovations is a leading organization in the Pharmaceuticals industry that partnered with OMG Consulting to blockchain. The project was conducted between 2022-07-01 and 2023-05-31 with a budget of 2100000-3400000.

## Team Composition

OMG Consulting deployed a cross-functional team of 4 consultants to execute this project.

## Key Challenges

The project presented several significant challenges:

- Significant technical debt and outdated infrastructure
- Aggressive timeline with limited resources and competing priorities
- Resistance to change from key stakeholders and end users
- Integration with multiple third-party systems with limited API support

## Outcomes and Business Impact

The project delivered substantial value to Pharmaceutical Innovations:

- Implemented automated workflows that reduced manual processes by 80%, freeing staff for higher-value work
- Migrated 70TB of data to the cloud, reducing storage costs by 56% while improving accessibility
- Expanded digital capabilities, resulting in 41% increase in digital channel adoption

## Lessons Learned and Best Practices

This engagement yielded valuable insights that informed OMG Consulting's approach to future blockchain projects:

- Building a robust communication framework early in the project was critical to success
- Implementing a phased approach allowed for incremental value delivery while managing risk
- Establishing clear governance and decision-making processes helped navigate complex stakeholder requirements
- Investing in knowledge transfer and documentation ensured sustainable long-term value for the client
